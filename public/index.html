🚫 Why this tool shows zero regular funds — and why you should never invest in one
Our editorial position. We stand by it completely.

This tool shows Direct Growth plans only. No exceptions. Every Regular plan has an identical Direct counterpart — same fund manager, same stocks — but costs 0.5% to 1.5% more per year as commission paid to whoever sold it to you.

The maths is brutal. ₹10 lakh, 20 years, 12% gross returns. Direct at 0.5%: ₹89.2 lakh. Regular at 1.5%: ₹73.7 lakh. That's ₹15.5 lakh gone — not to markets, to a salesperson.

If you are in Regular plans right now: switch today. Most platforms allow direct switching without redemption. If you want advice, hire a SEBI-registered RIA — they charge a flat fee and are legally required to recommend Direct plans.
